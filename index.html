EmaraTech

A real-time VR platform that turns architectural models into spaces you can walk through, built as my capstone and chosen the best in my department.

  • Best Capstone Design ProjectComputer Engineering department, Kuwait University · KFAS, 2026
  • Digital Transformation AwardKuwait Petroleum Corporation (KPC), 49th Engineering Design Exhibition, January 2026
  • Digital Transformation ExhibitionRepresented Kuwait University under the patronage of the Minister of Oil, January 2026

Architects and clients usually review a design through drawings and screenshots. EmaraTech lets them step inside it instead. It takes a Revit model, converts it to a VR environment in seconds, and keeps everyone in the same live view as the design changes.

I worked on model processing, live synchronization and user interaction. At the expo, the people we spoke to were most interested in how fast the conversion is and in the electrical, plumbing and HVAC plans the system generates automatically.

  • VR
  • Unity
  • C#
  • Revit
  • Real-time sync

What it does

  • Revit-to-VR conversion in seconds
  • Cost estimation
  • Carbon footprint estimate
  • Automatic electrical, plumbing and HVAC planning
  • Blockchain logging of changes
